# [[547,8,21]] — single-layer weight-8 tile code (3 x 3 tile, 15 x 15 bulk), reduced by row-space grafts

## Direction & hypothesis

The X-tile {h00, h01, h11, h22, v02, v10, v20, v21} of `codes/288-8-15.json` and `codes/338-8-16.json` (ours) gains
distance steadily with the bulk, and `codes/800-8-27.json` built it at 20 x 20. Between n = 338 and 796 the
single-layer weight-8 cell at k = 8 held only weight-6 codes, so we built the tile at the sizes in between, then
shrank each with row-space grafts.

## What was searched

- The tile on square bulks 12 x 12 to 17 x 17 (n = 392 to 722), the paper's open boundary (Steffan, Choe, Breuckmann,
  Pereira, Eberhardt, arXiv:2504.09171). On 15 x 15 it gives [[578,8,21]].
- Row-space grafts, the move @dorakingx introduced on this board (`notes/280-8-15.md`, used there for
  `codes/280-8-15.json` and `codes/316-8-16.json`): S a sum of at most three checks, the qubit disentangled by a CNOT
  fan-out and deleted, at unchanged k, weight 8 and radius 4.0. Each graft had to survive this repository's
  bit-packed RIS (`verify/gf2_fast.cpp`) at 20,000 trials and twice at 200,000 trials on hashed seeds. The chain
  reached n = 541; a final 2 x 1,000,000-trial check failed there, and bisection over the accepted chain kept the
  first 31 grafts, n = 547.

## Evidence trail

- Fresh-seed RIS ladder on the reduced code, each rung on its own seed: 21 at 20k, 200k, 1M and 5M trials, nothing lighter. The distance
  is a witness-backed upper bound, not a proof.
- Not dominated in weight-8 x local-2d-single: the best k = 8 entry at n <= 547 is `codes/511-8-20.json` (d 20,
  ours). This code dominates the ungrafted [[578,8,21]] from the same tile (ours, PR #3586).

## Dead ends

- Above n = 500 the per-step filter keeps passing grafts that a longer search then refutes: at 14 x 14 the chain
  rolled back to a single graft, here to 31 of 37. The final check with rollback is what keeps the claim honest.

## Tools

Claude Opus 5.5 in Claude Code; our research package (tile construction, graft, RIS ladder); this repository's
`verify/gf2_fast.cpp` (RIS), `cli/qldpc.py` and `verify/`.

## Reproduction

B = 3, X-tile {h00, h01, h11, h22, v02, v10, v20, v21} where h(x, y) joins vertices (x, y) and (x + 1, y) and
v(x, y) joins (x, y) and (x, y + 1); Z-tile by T2 (h(x, y) <-> v(2 - x, 2 - y)); the paper's boundary on a 15 x 15
bulk, then the grafts above. The reduced checks and the coordinates (each surviving qubit keeps its site on the
45-degree rotated lattice) are in the submission JSON.
