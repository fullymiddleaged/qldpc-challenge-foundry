# [[360,8,17]] — single-layer weight-8 tile code (3 x 3 tile, 12 x 12 bulk), reduced by row-space grafts

## Direction & hypothesis

The X-tile {h00, h01, h11, h22, v02, v10, v20, v21} of `codes/288-8-15.json` and `codes/338-8-16.json` (ours) gains
distance steadily with the bulk, and `codes/800-8-27.json` built it at 20 x 20. Between n = 338 and 796 the
single-layer weight-8 cell at k = 8 held only weight-6 codes (d 17 at n = 454), so we built the tile at the sizes in
between. @dorakingx then showed (`notes/280-8-15.md`, `codes/280-8-15.json`, `codes/316-8-16.json`) that
row-space grafts delete qubits from these codes at unchanged k, weight and radius; we applied that move here.

## What was searched

- The tile on square bulks 12 x 12 to 17 x 17 (n = 392 to 722), the paper's open boundary (Steffan, Choe, Breuckmann,
  Pereira, Eberhardt, arXiv:2504.09171). On 12 x 12 it gives [[392,8,17]].
- Row-space grafts to a fixpoint, as `notes/280-8-15.md` describes: S a sum of at most three checks with a connected
  overlap pattern, lightest first, X side then Z side; the qubit is disentangled by the CNOT fan-out and deleted;
  every touched row checked against weight 8 and radius 4.0 in the source layout. 32 grafts were accepted
  (|S| = 1: 3, 2: 5, 3: 7, 4: 6, 5: 2, 6: 1, 7: 1, 8: 7), taking n from 392 to 360.
- Each graft with |S| >= 2 had to survive RIS at 20,000 trials and then twice at 200,000 trials with fresh seeds
  (this repository's bit-packed engine, `verify/gf2_fast.cpp`). Those rungs are a filter, not the evidence.

## Evidence trail

- Fresh-seed RIS ladder on the reduced code with the same engine: 17 at 20,000, 200,000, 1,000,000 and 5,000,000 trials, each rung on its own seed, nothing lighter. The distance is a witness-backed upper
  bound, not a proof.
- `verify/validate_candidate.py` passes (`passed: true`): it advances the weight-8 x local-2d-single board and is not a duplicate of any board entry.
- Not dominated in weight-8 x local-2d-single: the nearest k = 8 entries are `codes/316-8-16.json` (d 16) and
  `codes/454-8-17.json` (d 17, weight 6).

## Dead ends

- A first graft pass that screened each step at 2,000 then 20,000 trials stopped at n = 375, and that code fell to
  d <= 16 at 200,000 trials: one accepted graft had cost distance. With the 20k + 2 x 200k filter the pass rejects
  such steps and also reaches a smaller n.

## Tools

Claude Opus 5.5 in Claude Code; our research package (tile construction, graft, RIS ladder); this repository's
`verify/gf2_fast.cpp` (RIS), `cli/qldpc.py` and `verify/`.

## Reproduction

B = 3, X-tile {h00, h01, h11, h22, v02, v10, v20, v21} where h(x, y) joins vertices (x, y) and (x + 1, y) and
v(x, y) joins (x, y) and (x, y + 1); Z-tile by T2 (h(x, y) <-> v(2 - x, 2 - y)); the paper's boundary on a 12 x 12
bulk, then the grafts above. The reduced checks and the coordinates (each surviving qubit keeps its site on the
45-degree rotated lattice) are in the submission JSON.
