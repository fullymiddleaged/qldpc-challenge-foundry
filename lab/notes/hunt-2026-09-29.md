## What it showed

- Weight-8 BB codes do not lay out under the bilayer cap with our annealer: best 8.6 over 40 tries. The bilayer cap
  was set for planar tile codes (TRACKS.md: bulk checks about 5.83, corners 6.71), so weight-8 bilayer entries should
  come from tile codes (arXiv:2504.09171), not BB tori.
- Covers keep k (as arXiv:2511.13560 proves) but their Tanner graph wraps a longer torus the annealer cannot fold
  under 7; the sign-preserving x-cover of 216-8-16 gave [[432,8,<=20]] at radius 9.06 even with 3x the steps.
- Weight-6 BB on thin tori (m = 6) is where layouts succeed: all 19 successes were l x 6. None of the 12x8..20x8
  draws reached the proof stage.
- The screen's 100-trial bound overstated d on many w8 codes ([[288,4,<=34]]); the 1,000-trial refine step before
  layout is needed.
- DistQLDPC did not finish 4 of 10 proofs in 40 min per cube: three [[216,4]] and one [[288,4]] (both n > 200 with
  d_ub >= 18). Depth-2 orbital cubes gave only two cubes for these codes (the stabiliser of a point is trivial).
